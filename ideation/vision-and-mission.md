VISION
To reach the lost, restore the backslider, and to equip the believer in the basic principles of Jesus Christ. Turn the heart of fathers,mothers, youth, and children to Christ . Experience the manifestation of the promises according to scriptures.

A body of believers that walks up rightly consistently growing in Christ being a living example of the word of God laying foundation in the untouched areas of the world developing church leaders of tomorrow by teaching basic principals of salvation as we go where there is no path and leave a trail.
